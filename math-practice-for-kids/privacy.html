Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Math Practice for Kids is a math practice app for children. It is built to collect as little as possible: it has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, and no third-party analytics or tracking. This policy explains what that means in practice.

Information we collect

None. We do not ask for a name, email address, phone number, date of birth, location, photo, or any other personal information. There is no account to create.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your completed workouts (the questions, your answers, scores and times), your progress statistics, and your preferences (appearance and language). This data is stored only on your device using the operating system's local storage. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

No tracking, no ads

The app contains no advertising and no analytics, attribution, or tracking software. We do not build profiles of users and we do not sell or share data, because we do not collect any.

Network use

The core app works fully offline. The app only opens an external service when you deliberately choose to: rating the app opens your device app store, and "Share App" opens your device share sheet. In both cases the other service has its own privacy policy.

Worksheets and sharing

Printable worksheets are generated on your device. If you tap Share, Save or Print, the resulting PDF is handed to your device — you choose where it goes. We never receive it.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for children and is designed to comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation, including provisions for children (GDPR-K), by not collecting personal information from anyone, regardless of age.

Deleting your data

You can erase all stored workouts and progress at any time from Settings → Clear Workout History. Uninstalling the app removes everything it has stored.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will ship with a new app update and the "Last updated" date above will change.
