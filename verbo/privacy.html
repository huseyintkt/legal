Verbo – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

In short

Verbo is an Italian verb trainer that works offline. We have no servers and no accounts, and we don’t collect your personal data ourselves. The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which processes some device data as described below, only within the choices you make.

What stays on your device

Your settings, optional name, favorites, learned verbs and study history are stored only in the app’s storage on your device. We never receive them. They are deleted when you uninstall the app or use “Reset progress”.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep Verbo free, the app shows banner ads, occasional full-screen ads between study sessions, and optional rewarded ads that you can choose to watch for a period without ads. These ads are served by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.).

When an ad is requested, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, only if you allow tracking; Advertising ID on Android),
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information such as model, operating system, language and app version,
  • ad interactions such as impressions and taps, and diagnostic data.

Google uses this data to deliver, measure and, where you have agreed, personalize ads, and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices and consent

Europe, UK and Switzerland: before any ads are loaded, the app shows a consent form from Google’s User Messaging Platform. You can accept or refuse personalized ads; if you refuse, you may still see non-personalized ads based on context. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy options.

iOS: the app asks for permission to track through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, your IDFA is not shared. You can change this later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device’s Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Backups

When you export your data, the app creates a file and hands it to your device’s share menu. Where it goes is up to you; we never receive it.

Pronunciation

Audio is produced by your device’s built-in text-to-speech engine. The app sends no text over the internet for this, and it never records audio.

Children

Verbo is a general-audience language app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that resulted in data being collected, contact us and we will help.

Changes

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect, and ask for your consent again where required.

Contact & support

Questions about privacy, feedback or a mistake in a conjugation? Write to h.tekatli.ch@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.
